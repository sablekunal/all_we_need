Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At All We Need (AWN_), we respect your privacy and believe in full transparency. As an open-source community directory for developer tools, we prioritize collecting as little data as possible while operating a reliable public resource.

1. Information We Collect

We use lightweight web analytics (such as Google Analytics / gtag) to understand aggregated traffic trends, popular project pages, and general device statistics. This analytics data does not track personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Open-Source Contributions & Suggestions

If you submit a pull request on our GitHub repository or share feedback via our public community suggestions form, any information you provide (such as your GitHub username, avatar URL, or submitted feature suggestions) becomes publicly accessible as part of the open-source repository and Hall of Fame contributors list.

3. Third-Party Links

Our project cards contain links to third-party developer tools and services. Once you follow a link to an external tool, their respective privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external platforms.

4. Contact & Modifications

Because All We Need is built in the open, any discussions or concerns regarding data practices or removal requests can be raised directly via our official GitHub Issues.
