How AI Detectors Actually Work (And Why They Trigger)

Many writers assume AI detectors check a central database of generated responses. In reality, detection algorithms run statistical heuristics that examine two fundamental linguistic attributes:

Heuristic 1

Perplexity

Perplexity measures how surprised a language model is by a sequence of words. Large language models choose mathematically probable next tokens. If your sentence follows predictable n-gram patterns, perplexity is low, causing detectors to flag it as synthetic.

Heuristic 2

Burstiness

Burstiness evaluates the variance in sentence length, rhythmic structure, and syntactic complexity. AI models write with remarkably uniform paragraph cadence. Real humans, by contrast, write in bursts — mixing short punches with complex compound thoughts.

The Flaw in Conventional Rewriters

First-generation paraphrasers like QuillBot replace individual words with obscure thesaurus alternatives (e.g., swapping "use" with "utilize" or "crucial" with "pivotal"). Because the underlying sentence blueprint remains predictable, Turnitin and GPTZero still detect the AI signature, while human readers are left with jarring, unnatural phrasing.
