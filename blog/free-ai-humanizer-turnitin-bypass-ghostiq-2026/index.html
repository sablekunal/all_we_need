What Is TruthDraft?

TruthDraft (truthdraft.com) is a web-based AI writing detector and AI text humanizer. It was previously known as GhostIQ and has been rebranded with a sharper focus: give anyone a fast, honest read on whether their text looks AI-generated, and offer a humanized revision for free.

TruthDraft is built for students, researchers, and professionals who need to inspect or revise short passages before submitting them — without paying for a subscription or handing over an email address.

📌 Note

TruthDraft results are estimates only. They can be wrong and cannot prove authorship. The humanizer edits may change meaning and do not guarantee any particular detector result.

Key Features

  • AI Detector — Paste text and get an instant estimate of how AI-generated it reads, scored as a percentage.
  • AI Humanizer — Rewrites your passage with natural phrasing, varied sentence structures, and idiomatic flow.
  • Side-by-Side Draft Comparison — See exactly what changed between your original and the humanized version before using it.
  • Zero Account Required — No email, no OAuth, no API key. Open the site and go.
  • 100% Free — No credits, no trial periods, no hidden tiers.

How to Use TruthDraft

  1. Open truthdraft.com in your browser.
  2. Paste your text into the input field.
  3. Click Detect to see the AI detection score, or Humanize to get a revised draft.
  4. Review the side-by-side comparison of original vs. humanized text.
  5. Copy the output and use it. Done.

Why TruthDraft Replaced GhostIQ

TruthDraft is the evolved, rebranded version of GhostIQ. The rebrand reflects a broader mission: instead of just stealth bypassing, TruthDraft focuses on transparency — showing users exactly what the AI detection score is and what the humanized draft changed. The name "TruthDraft" reflects that ethos: honest detection, honest revision.

If you previously bookmarked ghostiq.pages.dev, update your bookmark to truthdraft.com.

💡 Tip

TruthDraft works best on short to medium-length passages. For long documents, break your text into sections and process each one individually for better results.
