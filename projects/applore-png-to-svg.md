APPLORE's PNG to SVG converter traces an image into an SVG made of standard paths. The free trace runs in the browser with WebAssembly, so the image never leaves your device. There's no signup, no watermark and no limit on how many images you convert.

## Features

- Full color: each color region becomes its own vector path.
- Transparent areas stay transparent, so there's no white box behind a logo.
- Accepts PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, GIF and BMP, up to 20 MB.
- Controls for the number of colors, removing small specks, and smooth curves vs. sharp corners.
- Download the .svg or copy the SVG code, then edit it in Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape or any text editor.

# Limitations
- Gradients and soft shadows turn into color bands when traced. An optional paid AI conversion redraws them as clean shapes instead.
