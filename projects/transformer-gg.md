Transformer.gg is a marketplace for sponsored posts on X. Paste your website and get a list of X creators who fit what you sell, each with a suggested price. Send offers in a few clicks. Sending is free; your card is charged only when a creator accepts, and the money is held until the post is live with X's Paid partnership label and our team confirms it. Every offer gets a tracked link, so clicks and sales are credited to the creator who drove them. Creators keep 80%.

Run everything from the web dashboard or hand it to Claude, Codex or your own agent through our MCP server (`https://transformer.gg/mcp`), CLI or REST API. Public docs and CLI repo: https://github.com/NBV-AppFactory/transformer-mcp.
