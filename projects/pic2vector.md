Pic2Vector converts raster images (PNG, JPG, WebP) into clean, color-layered SVG directly in your browser. The tracing engine is based on the open-source vtracer (Rust) compiled to WebAssembly and runs in a Web Worker on your device, so files are never uploaded.

# Key Features
- **Local processing**: Tracing runs in the browser; no upload, no account.
- **Three trace modes**: Full color, black & white, and posterize (up to 32 colors).
- **Live controls**: Simplification, corner threshold and speckle filter update the preview.
- **Editable layers**: Hide, lock or delete color layers before export.
- **Free SVG export**: Standard SVG with no watermark, ready for Figma, Illustrator, Inkscape or cutting machines.
