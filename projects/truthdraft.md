## Overview

**TruthDraft** is a web-based AI writing detector and AI text humanizer. It helps you review an automated writing estimate and compare an edited draft with your original text — completely free, with no account required.

> [!NOTE]
> TruthDraft is for students, researchers, and professionals who want to inspect or revise a short passage.

## Features

- **AI Detector** — Scans your text and returns an estimate of how much reads as AI-generated. Results are shown as a percentage with color-coded confidence indicators.
- **AI Humanizer** — Rewrites your passage to use more natural phrasing, varied sentence structures, and idiomatic flow. The edited draft is shown side-by-side with your original so you can review every change before using it.
- **Zero Login** — No account, no API key, no paywall. Paste your text, click run, and you're done.
- **Draft Comparison** — See exactly what changed between your original and the humanized version so you stay in control of meaning and accuracy.

## How to Use

1. Go to **[truthdraft.com](https://truthdraft.com/)**.
2. Paste your text into the input box.
3. Click **Detect** to see the AI score, or **Humanize** to get a revised draft.
4. Compare the original and revised versions side-by-side.
5. Copy and use the output — no sign-up needed.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Important Disclaimers:**
> - Detector results are **estimates only**. They can be wrong and cannot prove authorship.
> - Humanizer edits **may change meaning**. Always review the output carefully.
> - Humanizer edits **do not guarantee any detector result** — results may vary across platforms.
