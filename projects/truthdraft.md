## Overview

**TruthDraft** is a high-performance, web-based AI writing detector and text humanizer workbench. Designed to eliminate false positives and rigid synthetic syntax, it analyzes AI-generated text and restructures phrasing from ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini with natural perplexity, burstiness, and human cadence.

Unlike clumsy synonym spinners that break sentence structure, TruthDraft performs intelligent flow restructuring, comparing original drafts with humanized versions side-by-side — completely free, with no account or subscription required.

> [!TIP]
> **Zero Login & 100% Free**: TruthDraft does not require account creation, credit cards, or subscription tiers. The entire workbench is accessible directly in the browser with unlimited text restructuring and detection.

---

## Key Features

- **Dual-Pane Interactive Workbench**: Real-time AI score telemetry, side-by-side original vs. humanized draft comparison, and instant diff inspection.
- **Accurate AI Detection**: Instant heuristic scanning providing an estimate of AI-generated content percentage with confidence indicators.
- **Natural Syntactic Flow**: Restructures monotonous cadence and predictable n-grams into organic sentence lengths and natural human phrasing.
- **Detector-Specific Calibration**: Pre-calibrated against leading detection platforms including Turnitin (v2.4), GPTZero Deep, and Originality.ai 3.0.
- **Privacy First**: No draft content is logged or permanently retained for training datasets. Your writing stays entirely confidential.
- **Zero Login Friction**: No sign-up modals, API keys, or paywalls. Paste your draft, analyze, and copy results immediately.
