YYLO Ledger is a Git-native task and Record store with a shell-friendly CLI. It is for developers, automation authors, and coding-agent workflows that need reviewable current state, append-only history, dependency-aware work, and bounded queries without making a database the source of truth. Ledger owns Records and task history; it runs alongside the YYLO CLI (coding-agent and task/merge/release orchestration) and YYLO Benchmark (evaluation runs and their private evidence registry) packages.
