# Why It's Great
Mind Elixir turns ideas into interactive mind maps in the browser, with an outline mode for rearranging thoughts, flexible tags for finding maps, and local storage so your data stays yours. It also supports MCP and AI integrations for AI-assisted brainstorming.

# Key Features
- **Mind maps**: Create and edit interactive maps in the browser.
- **Outline Mode**: Rearrange thoughts with a fresh perspective on your map.
- **Tags**: Organize and find mind maps with a flexible tagging system.
- **Local storage**: Your data stays local, with no ads and no tracking.
- **Export**: Export freely to multiple formats.
- **Free plan**: A free plan is available, with paid Annual and Lifetime upgrades.
