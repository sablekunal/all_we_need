CodeTrendy is a curated directory of newly launched startups, SaaS tools and indie products. Every submission passes a content check before it is published, and each approved product gets a permanent page with its category, tech stack, screenshots, reviews and a link to the site.

## Features

- Browse screened listings across 49 categories, including Developer Tools and AI & ML
- "Built with" pages group products by tech stack; AI builder pages group them by the AI tool used
- Weekly trending list, launch calendar, tags and curated inspiration collections
- llms.txt and a full markdown listing index for AI assistants, plus RSS feeds for new listings and the blog
- Founder guides on launching, AI search, llms.txt and schema markup

## Free tier

Browsing is free with no account. Founders can submit a product for free (permanent listing; requires the CodeTrendy badge on your homepage and goes live after at least 7 days). Optional one-time paid plans start at $19. No rankings or traffic are promised.
